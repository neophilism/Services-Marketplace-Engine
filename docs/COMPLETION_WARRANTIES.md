# Completion warranties and follow-up

SME-035 binds a provider-authored warranty to an accepted completion for one completed Job Room. Only the exact active provider membership recorded by the completion may create the immutable warranty. Terms are bounded printable text, coverage must end after creation, and coverage may not exceed five years.

The room's exact active customer may record a routine follow-up or warranty claim while coverage remains current. Each immutable record preserves the customer membership, warranty, room, kind, summary, and timestamp. This contract records evidence and does not adjudicate claims, alter completion, authorize payment, or perform reversals.

Tenant-scoped request keys replay only when every material field and current binding match. Cross-tenant identifiers, inactive or rebound membership, nonaccepted completion, noncompleted rooms, expired or contaminated warranties, audit failure, and write failure all deny uniformly.
